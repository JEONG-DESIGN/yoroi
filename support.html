Support

Questions about setting up Yoroi, or something that does not work as described.

Before you write, please check the questions in the documentation.

Open the support form

The form asks for your name, email, store address, what happened and on which page, and lets you attach screenshots. You will receive an email confirming that we have your request. We reply within two business days (Monday to Friday, Japan time).

Code changes, third-party apps and design changes are not covered by support. See the support policy.
